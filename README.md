### 👨‍💻 About Me
I work in the IT industry, and everything in my repositories is born out of personal curiosity and real-world needs. I build tools, scripts, and side projects primarily to solve my own everyday problems or streamline workflows—and share them here in case they make someone else's life a little easier too.

- 💡 **Philosophy**: Keep it practical, keep it simple, automate the boring stuff.
- 🛠️ **What you'll find here**: Weekend hacks, handy utilities, and personal experiments.
- 🤝 **Open Source**: Feel free to fork, open an issue, or submit a PR if you find something useful.
